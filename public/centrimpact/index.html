We provide the data-driven narrative to demonstrate the value of your community engaged scholarship.
Answering the Four Critical Questions of Engaged Research

What is our shared foundation?

The CEnTR*IMPACT report illuminates the degree to which community partners and university researchers are truly aligned on core components like goals, values, roles, and resources.

Our slope chart visualization reveals where your partnership is strong and where communication needs to be strengthened.

How are we working together?

Our Project Dynamics analysis provides a map for facilitating a more successful and fair partnership.

The rose chart offers a nuanced view of your project’s context, processes, and outcomes, showing a balanced scorecard of the partnership’s health.

How far can our impact spread?

We measure the potential for your project’s impact to spread through its social network.

The Cascade Effects model visualizes your reach with a racetrack plot across first, second, and third-degree connections, identifying opportunities to broaden your influence.

What have we produced?

We start with clear, direct indicators that capture the real-time contributions of your partnership—the partners engaged, hours invested, and resources co-created.

How the CEnTR*IMPACT Toolkit Works
1. Upload & Structure Your Data
  • 1. Upload & Structure Your Data
  • 2. Analyze & Visualize
  • 3. Generate Your Data-Driven Report
The CEnTR*IMPACT toolkit begins with your data. We provide templates to help you gather and format the key information about your project’s partners, goals, and resources.
Once your data is uploaded, the toolkit’s powerful analytics engine takes over. It runs the advanced analysis and generates the four key visualizations that bring your project’s story to life.
The final step is an automated report template that incorporates the data tables and visualizations into a Quarto markdown document  . This document provides the foundation for evaluation, planning, and next steps and can be exported to many different formats.
