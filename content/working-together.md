# OFFERING TWO — IMPACT IMPLEMENTATION
# ─────────────────────────────────────────
o2_eyebrow: "With the Tools"
o2_title: "Implementing CEnTR*IMPACT at Your Institution"
o2_lead: "CEnTR*IMPACT gives you the components. Implementation is about calibrating them to reflect what your institution actually values."
o2_sidebar_1: "The SCE formula's W_output term — the weight assigned to each output type — is where institutional values get encoded. Setting those weights is not a technical task. It is a values conversation, and it requires the right people in the room."
o2_sidebar_2: "We help you facilitate that conversation, document the decisions, and build the infrastructure to make them defensible to the audiences that will scrutinize them: P&T committees, accreditors, faculty governance, community partners."
o2_body_1: "This engagement helps institutions move from understanding what CEnTR*IMPACT measures to actually using it: for faculty evaluation, accreditation evidence, grant reporting, and community accountability."
o2_body_2: "We work with your team to configure the framework to your context, pilot it with a cohort of faculty, and identify where the existing system needs to adapt to recognize the full range of engaged scholarship your institution produces."
o2_deliverables_label: "What institutions gain"
o2_deliverables:
  - "A locally calibrated SCE weight structure with documented rationale"
  - "Pilot evaluation data from a faculty cohort"
  - "P&T policy language that can accommodate engaged scholarship"
  - "A roadmap for institutional adoption at scale"
o2_format: "Multi-session engagement over one semester. Can be scoped as a pilot with a small faculty cohort or a broader institutional initiative."

# ─────────────────────────────────────────
# OFFERING THREE — STRATEGIC PLANNING
# ─────────────────────────────────────────
o3_eyebrow: "Beyond the Tools"
o3_title: "Strategic Planning for Community-Engaged Institutions"
o3_lead: "Most strategic plans for community engagement describe aspirations. Fewer describe the operational infrastructure needed to realize them."
o3_sidebar_1: "A plan that names community engagement as a priority without aligning P&T systems, resource allocation, and partnership structures to support it is not a strategy. It is a statement."
o3_sidebar_2: "We help institutions move from statement to strategy, using the CEnTR* framework to ground planning in what the institution is actually doing, and building toward what it is genuinely committing to."
o3_body_1: "This engagement begins with a landscape assessment: using CEnTR*SEEK and CEnTR*MAP to understand the current state of engagement across your institution. That picture then becomes the foundation for planning that is operationally grounded, not just rhetorically ambitious."
o3_body_2: "We work alongside institutional leadership, faculty governance, and community partners to develop plans that are credible to all three audiences, and that build the longitudinal evidence base the CET is designed to track."
o3_deliverables_label: "What planning looks like"
o3_deliverables:
  - "A landscape assessment of current community engagement across institutional text and records"
  - "Stakeholder sessions with faculty, leadership, and community partners"
  - "A strategic plan with operational commitments and evaluation metrics"
  - "Integration of CEnTR* tools as the institutional evidence infrastructure going forward"
o3_format: "Semester-long or academic-year engagement. Scoped based on institutional readiness and goals."
